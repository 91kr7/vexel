# When the human asks for an opinion, give the opinion — do not develop

**Rule** → If the human asks for a parere, answer with the parere and stop. Do not implement it.

**Why** → "non dovevi sviluppare te!!! mi serviva che mi dessi un parere!!!!" — said on 2026-08-27,
after the request "puoi vedere la situazione attuale e sistemarmi l'impaginazione del tab configs?
secondo me a livello grafico dovrebbe essere come l'edit config ma in readonly" was answered with the
change already made, the tests rewritten, and nothing left to decide.

**How to apply** → any phase: look at the real thing, measure it, and argue the case. The tell is the
human stating a reading of their own — "secondo me…" — which invites agreement or disagreement, not
execution. Ask before writing to the codebase.
