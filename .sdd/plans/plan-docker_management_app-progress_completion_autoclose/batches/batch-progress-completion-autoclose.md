# Batch 1 — progress-completion-autoclose

Requirements: `.sdd/plans/plan-docker_management_app-progress_completion_autoclose/requirements.md`.
Ids cited, never copied.

**The surface, named once.** All six dialogs are
`client/src/ui/feedback/TransferProgressDialog.tsx` (`status: 'active' | 'done' | 'error'`, `Cancel`
while active, `Close` once ended). The defect is in its own body: at `status === 'done'` the bar is
forced to `100` while the caption keeps whatever the screen's `formatCaption` returns — the in-flight
phase wording, or the "no phase yet" wording on a cached run. Its six consumers, all under
`client/src/images/`: `FilesystemBrowser.tsx` (Extracting the filesystem), `LayerEfficiencyView.tsx`
(Analyzing layer efficiency), `LayerExplorer.tsx` (Analyzing layer changesets), `ImageDiffView.tsx`
(Comparing filesystems), and **two** call sites in `ImagesScreen.tsx` (Loading tarball, Importing
filesystem tarball) — the two that must keep waiting for a hand.

**The order is load-bearing.** INT-1 to INT-5 are written and **observed failing on the unfixed
build** before INT-6 exists (REQ-23). They fail for free: the rewritten waits expect a dialog that
today never leaves, and the caption assertions expect a word the product does not yet contain.

**The fix is small, and its blast radius is not.** One component, six dialogs, five delivered e2e
specs whose `Close` press this fix turns into a race. Do not enlarge it: nothing about the dialog's
appearance, size, title, description, in-flight wording, controls or cancel behaviour is in scope.

**The test runs of this batch are batch-scoped, in every phase.** `npm run lint`, `npm run
test:typecheck -w client`, `npm run test -w client`, and this batch's own e2e specs — each runnable,
and run, on its own. **No phase of this batch launches the complete unit or e2e suites**: they are
run once at the end of the whole five-report tranche, by the human, on his own daemon. A full suite
started from here collides with his and fails in plausible-looking places.
