# Batch 1 — switch-surface-stability

Requirements: `.sdd/plans/plan-docker_management_app-toggle_focus_scroll/requirements.md`. Ids cited,
never copied.

**The order is load-bearing.** INT-1 and INT-2 are written and **observed failing on the unfixed
build** before INT-3 exists (REQ-14). A check written after the fix, and never seen red, proves only
that it passes — which is exactly how this defect shipped.

**The fix is one declaration.** The switch's wrapper is not a positioned frame of reference, so its
absolutely-positioned hidden checkbox resolves against a distant ancestor: measured at 1346px away in
the "Run a container" sheet, and focusing it on a real click drags the sheet to `y=-1044`. The remedy
was injected live and took the gap to 9px. Do not enlarge it: nothing about the switch's markup,
appearance, hit area, focusability or accessible name is in scope.
